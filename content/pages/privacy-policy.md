Google Analytics may collect information such as:

- IP address (which may be anonymized where applicable);
- Device and browser information;
- Pages visited;
- Session duration;
- Traffic sources;
- General usage statistics.

This information is used solely to improve the platform and user experience.

For more information about how Google processes data, please visit:

- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245

You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the official browser add-on:

https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
