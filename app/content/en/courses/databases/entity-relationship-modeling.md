# Entity Relationship Modeling

Entity Relationship modeling is a way to represent the main entities in a system and the relationships between them.

It is often used early in database design.

---

## Entities and Relationships

An entity is a thing you want to store information about, such as:

- A customer.
- A product.
- An order.

Relationships describe how those entities connect.

---

## Why It Helps

Entity Relationship modeling helps teams:

- Clarify business concepts.
- Spot missing connections.
- Reduce design mistakes before implementation.

It creates a shared visual understanding of the data model.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Entity Relationship modeling helps translate business concepts into database structure.
- It makes relationships explicit before tables are created.
- Good modeling reduces later redesign effort.
- Visual clarity improves collaboration between technical and non-technical stakeholders.
