# Joins

Joins combine data from multiple tables based on related columns.

They are one of the most important features of relational databases.

---

## Why Joins Matter

Because data is often normalized across several tables, important information is rarely stored in just one place.

Joins allow you to answer questions such as:

- Which customers placed which orders?
- Which students are enrolled in which courses?
- Which products belong to which categories?

---

## Common Join Types

Common join types include:

- Inner join.
- Left join.
- Right join.
- Full outer join.

Each type determines how unmatched rows are handled.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Joins connect related data across tables.
- They are essential in normalized relational schemas.
- Different join types produce different result shapes.
- Understanding joins is fundamental to practical SQL querying.
