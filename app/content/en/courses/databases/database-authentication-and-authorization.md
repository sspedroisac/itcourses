# Database Authentication and Authorization

Database security starts with controlling who can connect and what they are allowed to do.

Authentication and authorization are central to that control.

---

## Authentication

Authentication answers the question:

Who is trying to access the database?

This may involve usernames, passwords, certificates, tokens, or other identity mechanisms.

---

## Authorization

Authorization answers the question:

What is this user or system allowed to do?

Permissions may include reading data, writing data, creating tables, or administering the system.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Authentication verifies identity.
- Authorization defines allowed actions.
- Strong access control reduces security risk.
- Database permissions should follow least privilege whenever possible.
