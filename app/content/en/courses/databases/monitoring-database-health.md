# Monitoring Database Health

Databases need ongoing monitoring to remain reliable and performant.

Monitoring helps teams detect issues early and respond before users are heavily affected.

---

## What Teams Monitor

Common database metrics include:

- Query latency.
- Error rates.
- Connection counts.
- CPU and memory usage.
- Storage growth.
- Replication lag.

These signals help identify performance or reliability risks.

---

## Why It Matters

Without monitoring, problems may go unnoticed until they become visible outages or serious slowdowns.

Observability is part of responsible database operations.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Monitoring helps teams see how a database behaves in production.
- Metrics reveal performance and stability issues.
- Early detection reduces incident severity.
- Healthy operations depend on visibility.
