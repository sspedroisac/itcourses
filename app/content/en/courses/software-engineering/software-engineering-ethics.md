# Software Engineering Ethics

Software engineering decisions can affect privacy, safety, fairness, access, and trust.

Ethics in software engineering means considering the human impact of technical choices.

---

## Why Ethics Matters

Software can influence:

- Who gets access to services.
- How personal data is handled.
- Whether systems reinforce bias.
- How transparent important decisions are.

Technical choices are rarely value-neutral.

---

## Ethical Questions Engineers May Face

Engineers may need to ask:

- Is this data collection justified?
- Could this design exclude or harm users?
- Are risks being communicated honestly?
- Are safety and privacy being treated seriously?

These questions are part of professional responsibility.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Software engineering has ethical consequences.
- Engineers should consider user impact, fairness, privacy, and safety.
- Responsible engineering includes more than technical correctness.
- Trust is built through thoughtful and accountable decisions.
