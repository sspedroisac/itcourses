# Managing Scope

Scope defines the boundaries of what a project or feature will include.

Managing scope is essential because software projects can grow quickly as new requests, ideas, and edge cases appear.

---

## Scope Creep

**Scope creep** happens when extra work is added without proper review of impact.

This can lead to:

- Missed deadlines.
- Lower quality.
- Team burnout.
- Unclear priorities.

Scope creep is often caused by many small changes rather than one large decision.

---

## Managing Scope Effectively

Teams manage scope by:

- Defining clear goals.
- Writing explicit requirements.
- Breaking work into smaller increments.
- Reviewing change requests.
- Separating must-have items from nice-to-have items.

---

## Saying No or Not Yet

Managing scope does not mean rejecting good ideas forever.

It means deciding whether an idea belongs:

- In the current release.
- In a future iteration.
- In a different project.

This keeps delivery focused and realistic.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Scope defines what work is included.
- Uncontrolled scope changes create delivery risk.
- Good teams review changes intentionally rather than accepting them by default.
- Clear boundaries improve focus, predictability, and quality.
