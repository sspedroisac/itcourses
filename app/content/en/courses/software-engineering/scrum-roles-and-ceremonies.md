# Scrum Roles and Ceremonies

Scrum is a common Agile framework for organizing work in short iterations called sprints.

It defines a small set of roles and recurring ceremonies.

---

## Common Roles

Scrum often includes:

- Product Owner.
- Scrum Master.
- Development Team.

These roles help clarify responsibility for prioritization, facilitation, and delivery.

---

## Common Ceremonies

Typical Scrum ceremonies include:

- Sprint planning.
- Daily standups.
- Sprint review.
- Retrospective.

These meetings are meant to improve coordination, visibility, and learning.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Scrum organizes iterative delivery around specific roles and ceremonies.
- Its structure supports planning, visibility, and continuous improvement.
- Scrum works best when teams understand the purpose behind each practice.
- Meetings should help delivery rather than becoming routine overhead.
