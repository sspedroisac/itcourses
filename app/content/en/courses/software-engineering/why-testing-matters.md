# Why Testing Matters

Testing helps teams verify that software behaves as expected.

It reduces the risk of defects reaching users and gives developers more confidence when changing code.

---

## Benefits of Testing

Testing helps with:

- Catching regressions.
- Clarifying expected behavior.
- Supporting refactoring.
- Improving reliability.

Without testing, teams often become afraid to change code.

---

## Testing Is About Risk Reduction

Testing cannot prove that software is perfect.

Instead, it reduces uncertainty by checking important behaviors under realistic conditions.

The most valuable tests are usually the ones that protect critical flows or unstable areas.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Testing increases confidence in software changes.
- It reduces regressions and supports maintainability.
- Testing is a practical risk-management tool.
- Well-chosen tests make future development safer.
