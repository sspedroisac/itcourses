# Incident Management

An incident is an event that disrupts normal service or creates a serious risk to users or the business.

Incident management is the process of responding to, resolving, and learning from such events.

---

## Common Incident Activities

Incident response often includes:

- Detecting the issue.
- Assessing impact.
- Communicating with stakeholders.
- Mitigating the problem.
- Restoring service.
- Reviewing root causes afterward.

Clear roles and communication are especially important during stressful situations.

---

## Learning After an Incident

Good teams do more than restore service.

They also ask:

- Why did this happen?
- What signals were missed?
- What safeguards should be improved?

This turns incidents into opportunities to strengthen the system.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Incident management helps teams respond to service disruptions effectively.
- Recovery and learning are both important.
- Communication is critical during incidents.
- Strong post-incident review improves resilience over time.
