# Collaboration and Communication

Software engineering is a team activity.

Technical skill matters, but clear communication often determines whether work moves smoothly or stalls.

---

## Why Communication Matters

Teams rely on communication to:

- Clarify requirements.
- Coordinate changes.
- Explain tradeoffs.
- Share progress.
- Resolve blockers.

Miscommunication can create bugs, rework, and frustration.

---

## Good Collaboration Habits

Helpful habits include:

- Writing clear issues and pull requests.
- Asking precise questions.
- Sharing context early.
- Documenting important decisions.
- Raising risks before they become incidents.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Strong collaboration reduces engineering friction.
- Communication quality affects delivery quality.
- Clear written context is especially valuable in distributed teams.
- Good engineering includes making work understandable to others.
