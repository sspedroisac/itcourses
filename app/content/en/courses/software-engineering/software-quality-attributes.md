# Software Quality Attributes

Quality attributes describe the characteristics that make software valuable beyond its basic features.

They help teams define what “good software” means in practical terms.

---

## Common Quality Attributes

Important software quality attributes include:

- Reliability.
- Maintainability.
- Performance.
- Security.
- Scalability.
- Usability.
- Availability.
- Testability.

Different systems emphasize different attributes depending on context.

---

## Examples

- A medical system may prioritize safety and reliability.
- A streaming platform may prioritize scalability and availability.
- An internal business tool may prioritize maintainability and speed of delivery.

There is no universal ranking. Quality depends on the system's purpose.

---

## Tradeoffs

Quality attributes can compete with one another.

For example:

- Stronger security may reduce convenience.
- Higher performance may require more complex code.
- Faster delivery may reduce documentation quality.

Software engineering means making these tradeoffs intentionally.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Quality attributes describe how software should behave in real use.
- They are critical for architecture and planning decisions.
- Good teams define them early instead of treating them as afterthoughts.
- Engineering quality is about balancing competing goals.
