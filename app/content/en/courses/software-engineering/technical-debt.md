# Technical Debt

Technical debt is the future cost created by choosing a quicker or less ideal solution today.

Not all technical debt is bad, but unmanaged debt can slow development significantly.

---

## Examples of Technical Debt

Technical debt can appear as:

- Duplicated code.
- Missing tests.
- Outdated dependencies.
- Poor naming.
- Temporary workarounds that become permanent.

Over time, these issues make change harder.

---

## Strategic vs Accidental Debt

Sometimes teams intentionally take on debt to move quickly in a controlled way.

Other times, debt is accidental and comes from poor understanding, unclear ownership, or rushed delivery.

The key is to make debt visible and manage it deliberately.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Technical debt represents future maintenance cost.
- Some debt is strategic, but all debt should be tracked and reviewed.
- Ignoring debt can reduce delivery speed over time.
- Healthy teams balance progress with codebase sustainability.
