# Kanban Basics

Kanban is a workflow management approach that focuses on visualizing work, limiting work in progress, and improving flow.

It is widely used by engineering and operations teams.

---

## Core Practices

Kanban commonly involves:

- Visualizing tasks on a board.
- Defining workflow stages.
- Limiting work in progress.
- Measuring cycle time and flow.

This helps teams identify bottlenecks.

---

## Why It Helps

When too much work is started at once, progress slows and context switching increases.

Kanban encourages teams to finish work more consistently before starting new tasks.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Kanban focuses on visibility and flow.
- Limiting work in progress helps reduce overload.
- It is useful for continuous delivery environments.
- Small process improvements can create significant gains over time.
