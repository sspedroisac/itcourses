# Unit Testing

Unit tests verify the behavior of small pieces of code in isolation, such as functions, classes, or modules.

They are one of the fastest ways to validate logic.

---

## What Unit Tests Focus On

Unit tests usually check:

- Inputs and outputs.
- Edge cases.
- Business rules.
- Error handling.

Because they are small and focused, they are often quick to run.

---

## Benefits

Unit tests help teams:

- Catch logic errors early.
- Document expected behavior.
- Refactor with more confidence.

They are especially useful in code with clear boundaries and deterministic behavior.

---

## Limitations

Unit tests do not prove that the whole system works together correctly.

They should be part of a broader testing strategy that may also include integration and end-to-end testing.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Unit tests validate small pieces of behavior.
- They are fast, focused, and useful for catching logic issues.
- Unit tests improve confidence during refactoring.
- They are valuable, but not sufficient on their own.
