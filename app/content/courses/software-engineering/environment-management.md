# Environment Management

Software usually runs in multiple environments, such as development, testing, staging, and production.

Managing these environments well helps teams reduce surprises during release.

---

## Why Environments Differ

Different environments support different goals:

- Development for building features.
- Testing for validation.
- Staging for pre-release checks.
- Production for real users.

Problems arise when environments behave very differently from each other.

---

## Good Practices

Useful environment management practices include:

- Keeping configuration explicit.
- Avoiding manual hidden setup.
- Managing secrets securely.
- Reducing differences between staging and production.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Environments support different stages of delivery.
- Configuration and consistency are critical.
- Fewer surprises in production usually start with better environment management.
- Reliable releases depend on predictable runtime conditions.
