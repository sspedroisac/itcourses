# Release Management

Release management is the process of planning, coordinating, and delivering software changes to users.

Its goal is to make releases predictable and safe.

---

## Why Release Management Matters

Even good code can cause problems if released poorly.

Release management helps teams coordinate:

- Timing.
- Validation.
- Communication.
- Rollback plans.

This reduces deployment risk.

---

## Common Practices

Teams may use:

- Release checklists.
- Deployment windows.
- Feature flags.
- Canary releases.
- Rollback procedures.

These practices create control without blocking progress.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Release management reduces the risk of shipping changes.
- Good releases are planned, observable, and reversible.
- Smaller releases are often easier to manage safely.
- Delivery quality depends on more than just code quality.
