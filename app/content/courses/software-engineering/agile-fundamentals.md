# Agile Fundamentals

Agile is a family of approaches that emphasizes iterative delivery, collaboration, adaptability, and continuous feedback.

It is often contrasted with rigid long-term planning, but its real purpose is to help teams learn and respond effectively.

---

## Core Ideas

Agile thinking values:

- Delivering working software frequently.
- Responding to change.
- Collaborating closely with stakeholders.
- Improving through feedback.

Agile is more about principles and habits than ceremonies alone.

---

## Why Teams Use Agile

Software projects often involve uncertainty.

Teams rarely know everything at the start, so shorter planning and delivery cycles help them adapt as they learn.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Agile emphasizes iterative delivery and feedback.
- It helps teams adapt to changing information.
- Agile practices should support real learning, not become empty rituals.
- The goal is better outcomes, not process for its own sake.
