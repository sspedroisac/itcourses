# Relationships Between Tables

Relational databases are powerful because they can connect data across multiple tables.

These connections are called relationships.

---

## Common Relationship Types

The most common relationship types are:

- One-to-one.
- One-to-many.
- Many-to-many.

Each type models a different real-world connection.

---

## Examples

- One user may have many orders.
- One profile may belong to one user.
- Many students may enroll in many courses.

Many-to-many relationships often require a join table.

---

## Why Relationships Matter

Relationships reduce duplication and make data models more expressive.

They also support accurate queries across related information.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Relationships connect tables in meaningful ways.
- One-to-one, one-to-many, and many-to-many are core patterns.
- Join tables are commonly used for many-to-many designs.
- Understanding relationships is central to relational database design.
