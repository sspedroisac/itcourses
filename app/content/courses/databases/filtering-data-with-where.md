# Filtering Data with WHERE

The `WHERE` clause is used to limit results to rows that match specific conditions.

Filtering is essential when you want precise answers from a dataset.

---

## Examples of Filtering

Teams often filter by:

- User ID.
- Date ranges.
- Status values.
- Price thresholds.
- Text matches.

Without filters, queries may return far more data than needed.

---

## Why Filtering Matters

Filtering improves:

- Relevance of results.
- Query efficiency.
- Application correctness.

Careful conditions help ensure software uses the right data.

---

## Key Takeaways

- `WHERE` is used to restrict query results.
- Good filtering improves accuracy and efficiency.
- Conditions should reflect clear business rules.
- Filtering is a core part of practical SQL usage.
