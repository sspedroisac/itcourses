# Graph Databases

Graph databases are designed to store and query highly connected data.

Instead of focusing mainly on tables, they focus on entities and the relationships between them.

---

## Good Use Cases

Graph databases are often useful for:

- Social networks.
- Recommendation systems.
- Fraud detection.
- Knowledge graphs.

These problems often involve traversing many relationships efficiently.

---

## Why They Differ

In some relational systems, deeply connected queries can become complex or expensive.

Graph databases are built to make relationship traversal a first-class operation.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Graph databases specialize in connected data.
- They are useful when relationships are central to the problem.
- They offer a different model from tabular relational systems.
- Choosing them makes sense when graph traversal is a core workload.
