# Aggregate Functions

Aggregate functions summarize sets of rows into a single result.

They are commonly used in reporting, dashboards, and analytics.

---

## Common Aggregates

Examples include functions that:

- Count rows.
- Sum values.
- Calculate averages.
- Find minimums.
- Find maximums.

These operations help turn detailed records into useful summary information.

---

## Practical Value

Aggregate functions are useful for questions such as:

- How many users signed up this week?
- What is the average order value?
- What was the highest sales day this month?

---

## Key Takeaways

- Aggregate functions summarize groups of rows.
- They are central to reporting and analysis.
- Aggregation helps transform raw data into business insights.
- Careful query design is important when combining aggregates with grouping.
